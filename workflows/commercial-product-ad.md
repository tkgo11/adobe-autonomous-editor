# Workflow: Commercial / Product Promo / Paid Social Ad

## Trigger

Use for product promos, brand spots, direct-response ads, app promos, launch videos, paid social creatives, or conversion-focused commercials.

## Default assumptions

- every shot/graphic must support product understanding, brand perception, or conversion intent;
- show the product/benefit early when the brief does not call for a mystery reveal;
- maintain strict brand consistency when assets/guidelines are present;
- generate multiple internal hook/CTA variants when cheap to do so.

## Analysis priorities

- product visibility and hero shots;
- logo/brand colors/fonts provided in assets;
- spoken/written claims;
- demonstrations of features/benefits;
- CTA language;
- platform duration/aspect constraints;
- legal/disclaimer text supplied by the user;
- footage that can support alternate hooks.

## Edit plan

1. Define hook, problem/value proposition, proof/demo, brand reinforcement, CTA.
2. Build the shortest coherent path through those beats.
3. Prioritize clear product demonstration over decorative footage.
4. Add supers/callouts for key benefits when useful.
5. Build variant hooks or CTAs when the brief requests testing or when variants are inexpensive.
6. Preserve mandatory disclaimers exactly as supplied.

## Premiere rules

- use nested master content where multiple aspect/duration variants are required;
- keep text/disclaimer timing explicit and measurable;
- reserve dedicated graphics tracks for versioning.

## After Effects escalation

Use AE for:

- product beauty animation;
- tracked callouts;
- UI/device mockups;
- logo animation;
- advanced typography;
- cleanup/compositing;
- packshots/end cards.

## QC gates

Reject and repair if:

- product or CTA is obscured/unreadable;
- mandatory wording is altered;
- disclaimer duration is below a user-specified requirement;
- brand assets are distorted;
- visual claims contradict the supplied footage/brief;
- variant exports accidentally mix assets/text from different versions.
