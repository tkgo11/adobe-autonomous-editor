# Workflow: YouTube Long-Form / Editorial Video

## Trigger

Use for longer creator videos, essays, reviews, explainers, documentaries, commentary, or narrative YouTube content.

## Default assumptions

- prioritize coherent narrative structure and viewer comprehension;
- create clear sections/chapters internally even if chapter markers are not requested;
- vary visual treatment enough to prevent long stretches of static footage;
- do not sacrifice explanation quality merely to maximize cut frequency.

## Analysis priorities

Identify:

- thesis, setup, supporting points, examples, payoff/conclusion;
- chapter boundaries;
- duplicated arguments;
- weak tangents;
- b-roll needs and visual evidence requirements;
- opportunities for diagrams/screenshots/quotes/graphics;
- retention-risk stretches with little visual or semantic change.

## Edit plan

1. Build a narrative outline from the strongest available material.
2. Construct the primary dialogue/narration spine.
3. Remove repetition and tangents not serving the brief.
4. Add chapter-level visual motifs and transitions sparingly.
5. Cover abstract explanations with relevant b-roll, screens, diagrams, or graphics.
6. Maintain continuity in music and sound design across sections.
7. Create intro/outro only if they improve the requested product.

## Premiere rules

- organize bins by source role and chapter;
- use sequence markers for chapter boundaries and unresolved tasks;
- use nested sequences for complex chapters or reusable segments;
- maintain consistent track roles across the entire project.

## After Effects escalation

Use AE for:

- diagrams and explainers;
- quote cards/data visualizations;
- maps/timelines;
- branded section cards;
- tracked screen inserts;
- sophisticated intros/outros where justified.

## QC gates

Reject and repair if:

- narrative order becomes confusing;
- a claim loses necessary context due to trimming;
- chapter transitions feel inconsistent;
- b-roll contradicts spoken content;
- long static stretches remain where visual support is clearly available/needed;
- audio/music treatment changes abruptly between chapters without intent.
