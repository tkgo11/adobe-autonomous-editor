# Workflow: Event / Wedding / Travel / Sports Highlight

## Trigger

Use for event recaps, weddings, travel films, sports highlights, concerts, ceremonies, school events, or music-driven montage edits.

## Default assumptions

- prioritize emotional/visual arc over exhaustive chronology unless documentary coverage is requested;
- use music structure as an editorial guide, not a rigid beat-cut rule;
- preserve key moments and natural audio where they add emotional context;
- avoid repetitive coverage of the same action.

## Analysis priorities

- best shots by focus/exposure/composition/stability/emotion;
- duplicates and alternate angles;
- faces/important people;
- key event moments;
- camera motion direction and continuity;
- music beat/phrase structure;
- natural audio moments worth preserving;
- slow-motion suitability based on source FPS/shutter quality.

## Edit plan

1. Build an emotional or thematic arc.
2. Select technically strong and meaningful shots.
3. Group visually compatible movement and eyelines.
4. Align major transitions with musical phrases when appropriate.
5. Use speed changes/slow motion selectively.
6. Layer natural sound under music at important moments.
7. Use titles/location/date cards only when useful.

## Premiere rules

- organize by scene/location/time/event beat;
- avoid using stabilization on every handheld shot;
- never create fake slow motion from unsuitable footage unless interpolation quality is verified;
- maintain motion continuity across cuts where possible.

## After Effects escalation

Use AE for:

- stylized title sequences;
- tracked titles;
- cleanup/removal;
- speed-ramp support graphics;
- compositing or cinematic transition designs when justified.

## QC gates

Reject and repair if:

- key people/moments are omitted despite available coverage;
- the montage repeats near-identical shots unnecessarily;
- optical-flow/interpolation artifacts are visible;
- music cuts feel accidental;
- stabilization causes obvious warping;
- color mismatch between adjacent cameras is distracting.
